SKU 02 · Artisan Pastry Series

Traditional Chive Pockets 韭菜盒子

Premium Frozen · Street-Food Heritage · Pan-Fry Ready
Iconic Street Food High Demand Crispy · Flaky · Savory Chinese Chive · Egg · Glass Noodle Pan-Fry / Air-Fry
— Product Description

Few dishes carry the cultural weight of the 韭菜盒子 — the Traditional Chive Pocket. A staple of Northern Chinese home kitchens and street food stalls for generations, this half-moon pastry encases a fragrant filling of fresh garlic chives, scrambled egg, and silky glass noodles, seasoned with sesame oil and a whisper of white pepper. The result is a flavor combination that is deeply savory, subtly aromatic, and immediately recognizable to millions of Asian-American consumers.

— Culinary Profile

The pastry itself is the hallmark of this product. Laminated and lightly flaky, it crisps beautifully in a pan with minimal oil, developing a golden, lacquered exterior that gives way to a tender, steam-soft interior. Pan-fry for four minutes per side from frozen and the result is a product that looks — and tastes — homemade. Works equally well in an air fryer for a lower-oil, modern prep method that will appeal to health-conscious shoppers.

SKU 03 · Signature Pancake Series

Crispy Scallion Pancakes 葱油饼

Premium Frozen · Multi-Occasion Format · Individually Wrapped
Top-Selling SKU Multi-Occasion Crispy · Layered · Aromatic Scallion · Sesame · Sea Salt Pan / Oven / Air-Fry
— Product Description

The 葱油饼 is arguably the most accessible and universally loved format in the Asian frozen foods category — and our Crispy Scallion Pancakes are the best version of it available in the US market. Made with laminated, hand-rolled dough layered with fragrant scallion, toasted sesame, and a touch of sea salt, each pancake bakes into an extraordinary contrast of textures: shatteringly crisp on the outside, chewy and steamy within, with a savory aroma that will draw shoppers down the aisle.

— Culinary Profile

From frozen to plated in under seven minutes. Pan-fry in a lightly oiled skillet for a golden, crackly crust. Bake in a conventional oven for a more uniform crisp. Air-fry for a lighter result with exceptional texture. Serve as a standalone snack with dumpling dipping sauce, as a side to soups and noodles, or as an appetizer platter anchor. The scallion pancake crosses every meal occasion — brunch, lunch, dinner, and late-night snack — making it one of the highest-utility items a buyer can add to their frozen set.
