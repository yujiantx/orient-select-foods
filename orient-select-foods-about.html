Who We Are

The Bridge Between Two Food Worlds

The North American Asian food market has never been larger — or more competitive. Consumers are more sophisticated than ever, seeking out authentic dim sum, hand-folded dumplings, and traditional savory pancakes that taste like they came from a neighborhood kitchen, not a factory floor.

The problem for US buyers has always been the gap between that demand and dependable, compliant supply. Sourcing directly from overseas manufacturers means navigating FDA registration, customs clearance, cold chain logistics, and FSVP obligations — a burden that falls squarely on the importer.

"We built Orient Select Foods so that procurement teams could stop worrying about the supply chain and start focusing on what matters: putting great product on the shelf."

Based in Dallas, TX, we operate as a fully registered US FSVP Importer with an established cross-border supply chain connecting North America's finest Asian food manufacturers to retailers and distributors coast to coast.

Quality & Compliance

Our Regulatory Standards Are Not a Checklist.
They Are Our Foundation.

In B2B food distribution, a single compliance gap can shut down a buying relationship, trigger a product recall, or expose a retailer to serious liability. We have engineered our entire operation so that risk never reaches your desk.

🏛️
FSVP Importer of Record

Fully Registered US FSVP Importer

Orient Select Foods is a fully registered importer under the FDA's Foreign Supplier Verification Program — one of the most rigorous food safety frameworks in the world. We hold the legal responsibility so our buyers never have to.

Registered with FDA as the US Importer of Record for all product lines
Full FSVP documentation maintained for every foreign supplier in our network
Hazard analysis performed on each product category — on file and audit-ready
Supplier verification activities conducted prior to initial import and on an ongoing basis
🏭
Facility Certification

FDA-Registered &
HACCP-Certified Partners

We partner exclusively with manufacturing facilities that have earned and maintained the highest level of food safety certification. Certification is not a one-time requirement — it is an ongoing condition of our supplier relationships.

All partner facilities are FDA-registered under 21 CFR Part 1, Subpart H
HACCP (Hazard Analysis & Critical Control Points) plans active at every production facility
Regular third-party facility audits conducted and results available to qualified buyers upon request
GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) compliance verified across all manufacturing sites
❄️
Cold Chain Integrity

An Unbroken Cold Chain. From Origin to Your Door.

Temperature integrity is non-negotiable in frozen food distribution. We operate a fully documented, continuously monitored cold chain from the moment product leaves the manufacturing facility to the moment it arrives at your dock.

Maintained at ≤ -18°C / 0°F throughout the entire supply chain
Temperature-monitored refrigerated transport at every logistics stage
Cold storage facilities used at border crossing and domestic distribution points
Temperature logs available with each shipment for quality assurance records
🚢
Cross-Border Logistics

Canada-to-US Border Clearance. Handled.

Our cross-border supply chain is structured for speed, predictability, and full customs compliance. Buyers receive product on DDP terms — all import duties, brokerage fees, and US Customs clearance costs are borne by Orient Select Foods.

Licensed customs broker engaged on all cross-border shipments
DDP (Delivered Duty Paid) terms — no hidden charges passed to the buyer
Prior Notice filed with FDA on all imported food shipments per 21 CFR Part 1, Subpart L
Country of origin documentation and labeling compliant with US Customs and COOL requirements
