What DDP Means for Your Procurement Team

All orders ship on Delivered Duty Paid terms. Orient Select Foods covers customs clearance, import duties, and cold chain transport to your designated US warehouse. You receive one invoice. No brokerage fees. No duty line items. No cold chain gaps.
